Valley Christian Schools · Chandler, Arizona

Two teams. One family. Made for God, powered by God.

VCS Robotics designs, builds, and programs competition robots in FIRST Tech Challenge, and has since 2008. Our JV team trains the next generation of our varsity team, the 2015 World Champions.

The pathway

From first robot to World Championship

New members start on our JV team, +3 Robotics, and spend at least two years there before moving up to varsity, Valley X. Varsity members mentor the JV students coming up behind them, so the program keeps itself going year after year.

Made for God, powered by God.

The robotics program at Valley Christian Schools. Our JV team trains the next generation of our varsity team, the 2015 FIRST Tech Challenge World Champions.

  • 2015 FTC World Champions
  • Competing since 2008
  • 5 World Championship trips
  • 18 State appearances
Meet the teams Support the program

VCS Robotics designs, builds, and programs a new competition robot every season in FIRST Tech Challenge. Students start on our JV team, +3 Robotics, and move up to varsity, Valley X. Along the way they write open-source software used by teams across Arizona, and host the state's FTC Kickoff.

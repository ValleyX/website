Why sponsor VCS Robotics

This season we raised $35,500, enough to fund our entire season. Sponsorship covers what it takes to compete: FIRST registration, robot parts and materials, practice field elements, tools, and travel to events like State and Premier Events.

In return, you're backing students who are learning engineering, programming, and leadership on real deadlines, many of whom go on to study and work in STEM.

Ways to help

  • Financial support for registration, parts, and travel
  • In-kind donations such as machining, materials, software, or tools
  • Mentorship from engineers, programmers, and business professionals

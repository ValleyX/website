A team tradition

Arizona FTC Kickoff

For the third year in a row, we hosted and ran the Arizona FIRST Tech Challenge Kickoff, the event where teams see the new game for the first time. Every FTC team in Arizona, 86 in all, came to our 5–8th grade campus to see BIOBUZZ revealed.

We coordinate the space and the volunteers, plus breakout sessions and workshops, many hosted by other teams. This year's Kickoff had 52 workshop sessions, most with room for 20 or more people, including a design and strategy workshop led by our head coach.

Gracious professionalism

Helping teams at competitions

At the Western Edge Premier Event in California, we brought most of our tools and parts, and kept a board in our pit inviting other teams to ask. We gave away or lent out 37 parts and tools for quick fixes when robots broke down.

Closer to home, some of our members volunteered with setup and scorekeeping at an off-season event run by The Dragons (FTC 10523).

STEM summer camp

In June, some of our members helped run a STEM summer camp for elementary through junior high students, teaching them to build, program, and work through obstacles.

Interest meeting

Every fall, returning students host an interest meeting to introduce FIRST and our teams. This year drew a record 20 to 25 new students.

Open-source tools

ValleyLib, valleyVis, and Apex Scout are free for any team. Apex Scout alone has been used by more than 70 people on more than 10 teams. See our software.

Civil Air Patrol

We built a robotics session for Civil Air Patrol cadets: a robot demo, a walkthrough of design, sensors, and programming, and a chance to drive the robot.

An outside perspective

After every outreach event or presentation, we ask for feedback, to make sure our message makes sense to someone who knows nothing about FIRST or STEM.

Coming up

We're planning an outreach event at our junior high to bring FIRST to younger students, along with STEM classes at elementary schools.

Arizona FIRST Kickoff

For the third year in a row, VCS Robotics hosted and ran the Arizona FIRST Tech Challenge Kickoff, the event where teams see the new game for the first time. The latest one brought in 86 teams, which is every FTC team in Arizona, and included a workshop from our head mentor.

Helping teams at competitions

At the Western Edge Premier Event, we kept a request board in our pit and filled 37 part and tool requests for other teams. We've also volunteered at off-season events, including one run by The Dragons.

STEM for younger students

Our members teach at a STEM summer camp. Next, we're bringing FIRST to the Valley Christian junior high and to elementary STEM classes.

Civil Air Patrol

We've built a robotics session for Civil Air Patrol cadets: a robot demo, a walkthrough of design, sensors, and programming, and a chance for cadets to drive the robot. It ties robotics to aerospace, search and rescue, and remote piloting.

Open-source software

ValleyLib and Apex Scout are free for any team. Apex Scout has been used by more than 70 people on more than 10 teams. Read about our software.

Growing the program

Our latest interest meeting drew a record 20 to 25 new students, and our JV-to-varsity pathway turns them into the next generation of team leaders.

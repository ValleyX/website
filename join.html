Where you'll fit

Build

Turn CAD into a working robot: assembly, wiring, and repairs between matches.

CAD & design

Model every mechanism before it's built, and iterate on it after testing.

Programming

Write autonomous routines, driver controls, and our own software tools in Java.

Drive team

Drive, coach, and make strategy calls in matches.

Marketing & outreach

Run sponsor relationships, community events, and the team's story.

Portfolio & scouting

Document the season for judges, and gather data that shapes alliance picks.

How it works

Everyone starts on our JV team, +3 Robotics. After at least two years on JV, you're eligible for varsity, Valley X. Students also step into leadership roles (lead builder, coder, CADer, outreach, and captain) based on their strengths.

A season at a glance

  1. Aug–Sep
    Interest meeting & Kickoff

    New members join, the new game is revealed, and design begins. We host the Arizona Kickoff.

  2. Oct–Nov
    Build & iterate

    Prototype, test, and get the robot competition-ready.

  3. Nov–Feb
    Arizona qualifiers

    Compete at qualifiers across the state for a spot at State.

  4. Feb
    Arizona Championship

    The state's top FTC teams compete at State.

  5. Spring
    Premier Events & Worlds

    For teams that advance: Premier Events and the World Championship.

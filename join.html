Where you'll fit

Build

Turn CAD into a working robot: machining, assembly, wiring, and repairs between matches.

CAD & design

Model every mechanism before it's built, and iterate on it after testing.

Programming

Write autonomous routines, driver controls, and our own software tools in Java.

Drive team

Drive, coach, and make strategy calls in matches.

Marketing & outreach

Run our social media, sponsor relationships, and community events.

Portfolio & scouting

Document the season for judges, and gather data that shapes alliance picks.

A season at a glance

  1. Sep
    FTC Kickoff

    The new game is revealed and design begins. We host the Arizona Kickoff.

  2. Oct–Nov
    Build & iterate

    Prototype, test, and get the robot competition-ready.

  3. Nov–Feb
    Arizona qualifiers

    Compete at qualifiers across the state for a spot at State.

  4. Jan
    FRC Kickoff

    BLU CRU's season begins with a new FRC game.

  5. Feb
    Arizona Championship

    The state's top FTC teams compete at State.

  6. Mar–May
    Regionals & beyond

    FRC regionals, plus Premier Events and the World Championship for teams that advance.

A rookie season to remember

BLU CRU's first event was the 2022 Arizona Valley Regional. The team ranked 5th of 35, captained an alliance into the semifinals, and won the Rookie All Star Award, which earned a spot at the FIRST Championship in Houston that April.

What's next

--- layout: team team: "12841" title: FTC 12841 +3 Robotics description: >- +3 Robotics, FTC Team 12841, is the JV FIRST Tech Challenge team at Valley Christian Schools in Chandler, Arizona, and the feeder team for varsity Valley X. ---

Why a JV team?

A strong varsity team needs a pipeline. On +3, newer students do every job on a real robot: CAD, building, programming, driving, the engineering portfolio, and judging interviews. When they move up to Valley X, they already know how a season runs.

The two teams also compete side by side. At the 2026 Arizona Championship, Valley X chose +3 for its alliance, and the pair reached the Grand Canyon Division final together.

--- layout: team team: "12841" title: FTC 12841 +3 Robotics description: >- +3 Robotics, FTC Team 12841, is the JV FIRST Tech Challenge team at Valley Christian Schools in Chandler, Arizona, and the feeder team for varsity Valley X. ---

Two teams, one family

A strong varsity team needs a pipeline. Students spend at least two years on +3 before they're eligible for Valley X, doing every job on a real robot: CAD, building, programming, driving, the engineering portfolio, and judging interviews. Varsity members mentor and train the JV students, so the program keeps a steady flow of experienced people.

The two teams also compete side by side. At the 2026 Arizona Championship, Valley X chose +3 for its alliance, and the pair reached the Grand Canyon Division final together. Officially we're two teams, but we see ourselves as one team in Christ.

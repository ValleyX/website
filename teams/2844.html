The 2015 run

In the Cascade Effect season, Valley X went into alliance selection 4th in the Silicon Division at the West Super-Regional in Oakland. It was picked to join the 2nd-seeded alliance, reached the division final, and qualified for the World Championship in St. Louis. There, Valley X was the second pick on the Franklin Division alliance with 6433 The Neutrinos and 724 RedNek Robotics, which went on to win the 2015 World Championship.
